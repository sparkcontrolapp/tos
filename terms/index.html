Compatibility

Spark Amp Control requires macOS 27 or later. Hardware control has been tested with Spark 2. Compatibility with other models is not verified. Hardware functions require a supported, connected amplifier; demo mode simulates those functions and does not produce guitar audio.

Only one tone-control app can connect to the amplifier at a time. Operating-system updates, amplifier firmware, and changes to third-party services can affect compatibility.
