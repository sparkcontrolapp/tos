Privacy Policy

Your tone library lives on your Mac. Optional online services receive data when you use them. This policy explains the difference.

Effective and last updated: 29 October 2026 · Developer: Avner Barr

This policy covers Spark Amp Control (also called SparkControl) and this support website. For privacy questions or requests, contact avner.barr@gmail.com.

1. Data stored on your Mac

The app stores your tone library, groups, style tags, favorites, settings, and related editing data locally. It does not operate a SparkControl account service or upload your library to a SparkControl server.

Local aggregate counters record app launches and awake, foreground, connected, and demo duration. These counters do not measure guitar playing, record guitar audio, or include a user identifier. They are not automatically sent to the developer.

Demo mode uses sample data. It does not sign in, contact ToneCloud or Spark AI, or change your real library.

2. Optional Positive Grid services

When you use online features, the app communicates directly with Positive Grid. Sign-in sends the information you enter to Positive Grid. Session renewal, ToneCloud searches and downloads, and AI service-status checks also contact Positive Grid. These requests do not pass through a SparkControl server.

When you submit a Spark AI request, Positive Grid receives your text prompt and tone context: the selected result when refining a tone, or the current tone when starting a new request. Requests may contain tone names, model choices, and control settings. Avoid putting personal or sensitive information in a prompt.

Positive Grid’s Privacy Policy governs its processing, retention, service providers, and data rights. That policy states that submitted AI inputs and outputs may be used to improve its models. SparkControl does not control Positive Grid’s retention or training practices. If you do not want to send this data, do not use those optional features; local library use does not require them.

3. Credentials and Keychain

Your Positive Grid password is sent during sign-in and is not saved by SparkControl. Session credentials are stored in macOS Keychain on this Mac, with iCloud Keychain syncing disabled for that item. Sign out in Accounts to remove the app’s saved session. Signing out does not delete your Positive Grid account or data held by Positive Grid.

4. Bluetooth, audio input, and files

Bluetooth access is used to discover and control your amplifier. The onboard looper records and plays on the amplifier; it is not an automatic guitar-audio upload to the developer.

The optional USB guitar-processing workflow uses your selected audio input locally. Audio Input permission is not required for Bluetooth tone editing. The app does not automatically send that audio to SparkControl servers.

File import, export, and diagnostic export use files or locations you choose. You control where exported files are stored and with whom they are shared.

5. Optional local MCP access

MCP is disabled until you enable it in Settings. It listens on this Mac’s localhost address and requires an access token. A client with that token can read the exposed tone and device information and use the available control tools.

An assistant you connect may send tool results to its own provider, including a cloud service, according to its settings and privacy policy. A local server does not mean that your assistant’s processing is local. Connect only clients you trust. Turn off MCP to stop access, or create a new token to revoke clients using the old one.

6. Diagnostics and support

The app keeps up to six local Apple metric reports and six local diagnostic reports, with a 2 MB limit for each report. These can contain system metadata and stack traces. The app does not upload them automatically. Apple may separately collect crash and performance information under your system, App Store, or TestFlight sharing settings.

If you email support or choose to share a report, the developer receives your email address, message, and attachments. They are used to answer your request and investigate the issue, and are retained as needed for that purpose and any applicable legal obligations. Email is handled by the email service provider. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

7. This website

This is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not include analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, forms, third-party fonts, or application cookies. Your browser contacts GitHub to load the pages. GitHub may process connection information, such as an IP address, under its Privacy Statement. Following an external link opens a service with its own privacy practices.

8. Your controls and requests

  • Use the local library and demo without signing in.
  • Sign out of Positive Grid, disable MCP, and manage Bluetooth or Audio Input access in System Settings.
  • Remove tones and organization data using the app’s library controls. For help removing remaining local app data, contact support.
  • Ask the developer about access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence by email. Rights and exceptions depend on the laws that apply to you.
  • Contact Positive Grid, Apple, or your connected assistant provider directly for requests about data they hold.

SparkControl does not use an advertising SDK or sell your app data. We do not intentionally solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so we can address it.

9. Changes

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date. A change to the app’s data practices will be reflected here.
